Hi, I'm Azra — not your average CS major (I'm Engineering).

I study ECE + math at Rutgers ('27) and build ML systems at Google. Before that: NASA, Goldman Sachs, Columbia AI. Somewhere along the way I picked up a stubborn habit — I don't trust a result, including my own, until it survives an honest test.

### what I'm building

- [interp](https://github.com/azrabano23/interp) — ask your coding agent *why* a language model said that, and get measurements back — logit lens, activation patching, SAE features — checked against published circuits, on a laptop
- [evalkit](https://github.com/azrabano23/evalkit) — LLM evals with error bars: confidence intervals, unbiased pass@k, significance tests, and judges that can't be sweet-talked
- [cross-sae](https://github.com/azrabano23/cross-sae) — do vision models and the human visual cortex learn the same features? I'm building the statistics to answer that carefully
- with [@RyanRana](https://github.com/RyanRana) — **neural alloy**: what would it take to *trust* a fielded autonomous machine? Carbonium is our first answer, a fault-diagnosis engine that shows its evidence (co-contributor; private for now)

### what I care about

- **medicine that arrives on time** — [thaakat](https://github.com/azrabano23/thaakat) looks for endometriosis in pelvic MRI, a disease that takes years to diagnose; [aurelis](https://github.com/azrabano23/aurelis) grades clinical notes with judges checked against human graders
- **biological time** — I founded the Grey Matter Society at Yale School of Medicine, and I keep coming back to circadian rhythms: [circa](https://github.com/azrabano23/circa) · [rhythmrx](https://github.com/azrabano23/rhythmrx) · [fitra](https://github.com/azrabano23/alifsideprojectsnight)
- **honest measurement** — [vigil](https://github.com/azrabano23/vigil) and [steering-audit](https://github.com/azrabano23/steering-audit) both exist to report the true number, not the flattering one
